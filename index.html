About Food Rescue

Making food sharing easier and reducing food wastage.

Why Food Rescue?

Every day, restaurants and hotels have surplus food. At the same time, many people need nutritious meals.

Food Rescue provides a simple platform to connect food donors with NGOs and volunteers.

Our goal is to make surplus food useful and create a better community.
