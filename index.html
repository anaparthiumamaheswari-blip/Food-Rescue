About Food Rescue

The problem

Restaurants and hotels often cook more than they sell. Safe, edible food ends up in the bin while millions go to bed hungry.

How it works

A restaurant posts surplus food. An NGO accepts it. A volunteer picks it up and marks it collected. Status updates live for everyone.

Why it matters

Saving surplus food feeds people, cuts waste and reduces the greenhouse gases that rotting food produces.
